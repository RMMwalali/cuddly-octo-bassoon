Reactive Captions

Captions that sound like the people speaking

Drop a video. Each speaker gets a color, every word lights up as it's said, and the type gets bolder for lower voices, thinner for higher ones, and bigger when it's loud.

Options
Already have captions?

Your video stays on your device. In Fast mode only a compressed copy of the audio is sent for transcription and it is not stored. Pick Private under Options to keep everything on your device.

What makes these captions different

Most auto captions only show what was said. Reactive Captions also shows how: who is speaking, when each word lands, and how loud or soft it was. It follows the ideas of Caption with Intention, an award-winning caption design system made with the Deaf and hard-of-hearing community.

Is it free? Do I need to sign up?

You can try it free, with no account. Open the page, choose a video and you have captions in under a minute once the models are downloaded.

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

Your video never leaves your device. In Fast mode, a compressed copy of the audio track is sent to a speech recognition service to be transcribed in a few seconds, and it is not kept. In Private mode (under Options) transcription, speaker detection and rendering all happen on your own phone or computer, so nothing is sent at all.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes, in Safari and Chrome. Fast mode transcribes a typical clip in seconds. Private mode downloads a speech model once (about 80 MB, so use Wi-Fi) and works best with clips under 2 minutes on phones.

Which languages does it understand?

English, Swahili, French, Arabic, Spanish, Portuguese, Hindi, German and many more. Pick the language under Options for the best accuracy.

Can I post the result on TikTok, Reels, YouTube Shorts or WhatsApp?

Yes. Export the video with captions burned in, or export an .srt subtitle file to upload separately.

Are you part of Caption with Intention?

No. Reactive Captions is an independent tool inspired by the Caption with Intention design system from the Chicago Hearing Society. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by them.
